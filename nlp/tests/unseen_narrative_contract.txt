THIS AGREEMENT is made as of 15 March 2026 between Aurora Festival Group ("Client") and Horizon Sound Collective, with offices at 22 Tomas Morato Avenue, Quezon City, Philippines ("Vendor").

WHEREAS Client requires live audio reinforcement for outdoor festivals; and Vendor represents it can supply mix engineers and a public-address system.

The term begins 15 March 2026 and expires 14 March 2027.

Vendor shall provide front-of-house mixing, stage monitoring, and overnight equipment security for Client-produced festivals in Luzon, including load-in the day before each show and strike within eight hours after the last act.

The total consideration is PHP 1,975,000.00. Invoices are due net thirty (30) days from receipt.

If a show is delayed more than four hours because of Vendor equipment failure, liquidated damages of 3% of that event fee shall apply.

This contract shall automatically renew for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives sixty days written notice of non-renewal.

Either party may end this agreement for uncured material breach after thirty days written notice. Client may terminate for convenience with ninety days notice.

Governing law: Republic of the Philippines. Confidentiality lasts twenty-four months after termination.

Signed for Client by Andrea L. Fajardo, Festival Director, and for Vendor by Noel C. Ramirez, Technical Principal, on 01 March 2026.
