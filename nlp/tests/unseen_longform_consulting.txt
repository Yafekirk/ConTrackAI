PROFESSIONAL CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Professional Consulting Services Agreement (this "Contract") is entered into on 3 February 2027 by and between Sunrise Hotels Group, Inc., a corporation organized under Philippine law with principal office at 21st Floor, Robinsons Summit Center, Ayala Avenue, Makati City, represented herein by its Chief Financial Officer, Ms. Teresa V. Ocampo, hereinafter called the "Company"; and BrightPath Advisory Partners Co., a partnership with office address at 7F Net Lima Building, 5th Avenue, Bonifacio Global City, Taguig City, represented herein by its Managing Partner, Mr. Gabriel N. Soriano, hereinafter called the "Consultant".

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company operates eleven (11) hotels and resorts nationwide and intends to modernize its revenue management and procurement processes;

WHEREAS, the Company issued a Request for Proposal on 12 October 2026 and received proposals from five (5) firms;

WHEREAS, the Consultant was incorporated on 8 August 2014 and has completed similar engagements for hospitality groups in Southeast Asia;

WHEREAS, the Consultant's proposal dated 9 November 2026 was found to be the most advantageous to the Company;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Deliverables" means the reports, models, and training materials described in Section 2.

1.2 "Fees" means the professional fees payable under Section 4.

2. SERVICES

2.1 The Consultant shall provide revenue-management and procurement process consulting services to the Company, consisting of a diagnostic of current processes at all eleven (11) properties, design of a target operating model, configuration support for the Company's procurement system, and training of up to forty (40) Company staff.

2.2 The Consultant shall deliver a written diagnostic report within eight (8) weeks from the start of the engagement and a final implementation roadmap within twenty (20) weeks.

3. TERM

3.1 This Contract shall be effective as of 15 February 2027 and shall continue in force until 14 February 2028, unless terminated earlier under Section 9.

4. FEES

4.1 The Company shall pay the Consultant total professional fees of PHP 6,250,000.00, exclusive of value-added tax, plus reasonable out-of-pocket expenses not exceeding PHP 300,000.00 that are approved in advance by the Company.

5. PAYMENT

5.1 Fees shall be invoiced in four (4) milestones upon the Company's written acceptance of each Deliverable: 25% upon the diagnostic report, 25% upon the target operating model, 30% upon system configuration, and 20% upon completion of training.

5.2 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

6. INSURANCE

6.1 The Consultant shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than PHP 20,000,000.00.

7. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

7.1 If any Deliverable is delayed beyond its due date for reasons attributable to the Consultant, the Company may deduct liquidated damages of one-half percent (0.5%) of the Fees for each week of delay, up to a maximum of five percent (5%) of the Fees.

8. RENEWAL

8.1 This Contract may be renewed for a further period upon mutual written agreement of the parties executed at least thirty (30) days before expiry.

9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either party may terminate this Contract upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not cured within twenty (20) days after receipt of notice. The Company may terminate for convenience on forty-five (45) days written notice, paying only for Deliverables accepted up to the termination date.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party for three (3) years after the end of this Contract.

11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, and the courts of Makati City shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Contract on the date first written above.
