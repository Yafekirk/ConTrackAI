MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT FOR EVENT PRODUCTION AND TECHNICAL CREW SERVICES

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Master Services Agreement for Event Production and Technical Crew Services (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this 15th day of December 2026 at Pasig City, Philippines, by and between:

JBC Events Management, a corporation duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office at Unit 1203, One Corporate Centre, Julia Vargas Avenue, Ortigas Center, Pasig City, Metro Manila, Philippines, represented herein by its Chief Operating Officer, CARLO M. REYES, who is duly authorized for this purpose as evidenced by the Secretary's Certificate attached hereto as Annex C, hereinafter referred to as the "CLIENT";

- and -

Apex Production Crew Inc., a corporation duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office at 3rd Floor, Sagittarius Building, 111 H.V. Dela Costa Street, Salcedo Village, Makati City, Philippines, represented herein by its President and Chief Executive Officer, JEROME A. SANTOS, who is duly authorized for this purpose as evidenced by the Secretary's Certificate attached hereto as Annex D, hereinafter referred to as the "SERVICE PROVIDER".

The CLIENT and the SERVICE PROVIDER are hereinafter referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the CLIENT is engaged in the business of event planning, management, and production, and organizes more than one hundred fifty (150) corporate, social, and public events every year in Metro Manila and nearby provinces;

WHEREAS, the CLIENT requires a dependable pool of trained stage managers, riggers, lighting and sound technicians, and backstage crew to support the corporate conventions, product launches, and large social events it produces throughout Luzon;

WHEREAS, the SERVICE PROVIDER is a corporation duly registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission under Company Registration No. CS201512907 issued on September 14, 2015, and is engaged in the provision of stage management, rigging, lighting, sound, and backstage technical crew for live events, concerts, and corporate productions;

WHEREAS, the SERVICE PROVIDER represents that it possesses the technical capability, financial capacity, qualified personnel, licenses, and permits necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement, including a valid Mayor's Permit and BIR Certificate of Registration;

WHEREAS, on October 5, 2026, the CLIENT conducted a competitive canvass among no fewer than three (3) qualified suppliers, and the SERVICE PROVIDER submitted the lowest calculated and most responsive proposal dated October 19, 2026;

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of the CLIENT, in a resolution adopted on November 26, 2026, approved the award of this engagement to the SERVICE PROVIDER and authorized its representative to sign this Agreement;

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of the SERVICE PROVIDER, in a resolution adopted on December 2, 2026, likewise authorized its representative to sign this Agreement;

WHEREAS, the SERVICE PROVIDER has examined the requirements of the CLIENT, has satisfied itself as to the conditions affecting the work, and confirms that the price stated herein is sufficient to cover all its obligations;

WHEREAS, both Parties have conducted their own due diligence and confirm that no conflict of interest exists between them, their officers, and their employees with respect to this engagement;

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to set forth in writing the terms and conditions governing their relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants and undertakings hereinafter set forth, the Parties hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE I
DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings hereby assigned to them:

  - "Agreement" means this Master Services Agreement for Event Production and Technical Crew Services, including its Annexes, as may be amended from time to time in accordance with its terms.

  - "Applicable Law" means all statutes, ordinances, regulations, orders, and issuances of any governmental authority of the Republic of the Philippines that apply to a Party or to the subject matter of this Agreement.

  - "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or regular or special non-working holiday declared in Metro Manila.

  - "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or electronically, that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including client lists, pricing, event plans, and trade secrets.

  - "Contract Price" means the total amount payable by the CLIENT to the SERVICE PROVIDER under Article V.

  - "Effective Date" means the date on which the Term commences, as stated in Article III.

  - "Force Majeure Event" has the meaning given in Article XVII.

  - "Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade names, designs, know-how, and all similar rights, whether registered or not.

  - "Personal Data" has the meaning given to it under Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

  - "Personnel" means the officers, employees, agents, and subcontractors of a Party who are engaged in the performance of this Agreement.

  - "Term" means the period described in Article III, as may be renewed or extended in accordance with Article IV.

1.3 Headings are inserted for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. The words "include" and "including" shall be read without limitation. References to a statute include that statute as amended or replaced.

1.4 In case of conflict or inconsistency among the documents forming part of this Agreement, the following order of precedence shall apply: (a) this Agreement; (b) Annex A; (c) Annex B; and (d) the proposal of the SERVICE PROVIDER dated October 19, 2026, to the extent incorporated herein.

ARTICLE II
SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall furnish qualified stage managers, riggers, lighting and sound technicians, and backstage crew for events produced by the CLIENT, including technical rehearsals, ingress and set-up, show operation, and egress and strike within eight (8) hours after the end of each event, in accordance with the crew call sheets issued by the CLIENT.

2.2 For each event, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall assign one (1) crew chief who shall attend the CLIENT's production meeting not later than three (3) days before the event, confirm the crew list and arrival times, and serve as the single point of contact on site. The crew chief shall submit a signed event completion report, including attendance and incident logs, within two (2) Business Days after each event.

2.3 All riggers deployed under this Agreement shall hold current rigging and working-at-height certifications, and all electricians shall be supervised by a licensed electrical engineer. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall supply standard personal protective equipment, hand tools, radios, and safety harnesses for its Personnel.

2.4 The CLIENT may request additional crew or extended hours by written work order at least forty-eight (48) hours before the call time. Overtime shall be charged at the rates in Annex B and only when approved in writing by the CLIENT's production manager.

ARTICLE III
TERM OF THE AGREEMENT

3.1 This Agreement shall take effect on 1 January 2027 and shall remain in force until 31 December 2027 (the "Term"), unless sooner terminated in accordance with Article XVIII or extended in accordance with Article IV.

3.2 Obligations which by their nature are intended to survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement, including those relating to confidentiality, data privacy, indemnification, warranties, and payment of amounts already due, shall survive for the period stated in the relevant Article or, if none is stated, for the period allowed under Applicable Law.

ARTICLE IV
RENEWAL

4.1 Upon expiration of the Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods on the same terms and conditions, unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current Term. Rates for any renewal period may be adjusted only by a written amendment signed by both parties.

4.2 Any renewal or extension shall be documented in a written instrument signed by the authorized representatives of both Parties, and no course of dealing or acceptance of payment after expiration shall be construed as a renewal unless expressly provided in this Article.

ARTICLE V
CONTRACT PRICE

5.1 In consideration of the faithful performance by the SERVICE PROVIDER of its obligations under this Agreement, the CLIENT shall pay the SERVICE PROVIDER a total contract price of TWELVE MILLION FOUR HUNDRED EIGHTY THOUSAND PESOS (PHP 12,480,000.00), covering an estimated forty-eight (48) event days as detailed in Annex B (the "Contract Price").

5.2 The Contract Price is fixed and shall not be subject to escalation during the Term, except as expressly provided herein or as required by Applicable Law. The Contract Price includes all costs of labor, materials, equipment, transportation, overhead, profit, and taxes, other than taxes that are by law for the account of the CLIENT.

5.3 The SERVICE PROVIDER acknowledges that it has satisfied itself as to the sufficiency of the Contract Price and shall not be entitled to any additional payment by reason of any error or omission in its own estimates.

ARTICLE VI
PAYMENT TERMS

6.1 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall invoice the CLIENT after each completed event, attaching the signed event completion report, and the CLIENT shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) calendar days from receipt of a valid invoice and official receipt.

6.2 The CLIENT may withhold payment of any disputed portion of an invoice by giving written notice of the dispute within ten (10) Business Days after receipt of the invoice. The undisputed portion shall be paid when due.

6.3 All payments shall be subject to the withholding taxes required under the National Internal Revenue Code, and the CLIENT shall issue the corresponding BIR Form 2307 for amounts withheld.

6.4 No advance payment or mobilization fee shall be due under this Agreement.

ARTICLE VII
PERFORMANCE SECURITY

7.1 Within ten (10) days from signing of this Agreement, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall post a performance security in the form of a surety bond or irrevocable letter of credit in favor of the CLIENT in the amount of ONE MILLION TWO HUNDRED FORTY-EIGHT THOUSAND PESOS (PHP 1,248,000.00), equivalent to ten percent (10%) of the Contract Price, valid until sixty (60) days after the end of the Term.

7.2 The CLIENT may draw on the security to recover any amount due from the SERVICE PROVIDER under this Agreement, including liquidated damages, penalties, and the cost of remedying any default, without prejudice to any other remedy available to the CLIENT.

ARTICLE VIII
OBLIGATIONS OF THE SERVICE PROVIDER

8.1 In addition to its other obligations under this Agreement, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall:

  - Recruit, train, supervise, and pay its Personnel, and remit all mandatory contributions to the SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG Fund.

  - Comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Standards under Republic Act No. 11058 and the house rules of every venue.

  - Replace, within four (4) hours of a request by the CLIENT, any Personnel found to be unfit, intoxicated, or insubordinate.

  - Maintain accurate attendance records and make them available to the CLIENT for audit.

  - Keep all CLIENT equipment entrusted to its Personnel in safe custody and report any loss or damage immediately.

8.3 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall perform its obligations with the degree of skill, care, and diligence reasonably expected of an experienced and reputable provider of similar goods or services in the Philippines.

ARTICLE IX
OBLIGATIONS OF THE CLIENT

9.1 The CLIENT shall pay the Contract Price in accordance with Article VI, provide the SERVICE PROVIDER with timely access to its premises and venues as reasonably necessary, and designate a representative who shall coordinate with the SERVICE PROVIDER on all matters relating to this Agreement.

9.2 The CLIENT shall give the SERVICE PROVIDER reasonable advance notice of any change in its schedule, venue, or requirements that may affect the performance of this Agreement, and the Parties shall agree in writing on any resulting adjustment.

ARTICLE X
PENALTIES AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

10.1 For every day of delay in the arrival of the full crew complement at the call time stated in the crew call sheet, or for any event at which the SERVICE PROVIDER fails to deploy the full crew complement, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall pay the CLIENT liquidated damages equivalent to one-tenth of one percent (0.1%) of the Contract Price per day of delay, provided that the total liquidated damages shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the Contract Price.

10.2 In addition, any confirmed safety violation by the SERVICE PROVIDER's Personnel that results in a stop-work order at a venue shall entitle the CLIENT to a service credit of five percent (5%) of the fees for that event.

10.3 Liquidated damages and service credits may be deducted from any amount due to the SERVICE PROVIDER or charged against the performance security.

10.4 The Parties agree that the liquidated damages and penalties provided in this Article are a genuine pre-estimate of the loss likely to be suffered and are not a penalty in the sense of Article 1229 of the Civil Code of the Philippines.

ARTICLE XI
WARRANTIES AND REPRESENTATIONS

11.1 Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it is duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of the Philippines; (b) it has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; (c) the execution of this Agreement does not violate its articles of incorporation, by-laws, or any agreement to which it is a party; and (d) there is no pending or threatened litigation that would materially affect its ability to perform this Agreement.

11.2 The SERVICE PROVIDER further warrants that all goods, premises, or services furnished under this Agreement shall conform to the specifications in the Annexes, shall be fit for the purpose for which they are intended, and shall comply with all Applicable Law, including laws on labor, safety, health, and the environment.

11.3 The SERVICE PROVIDER warrants that it has not given or promised any commission, gift, or other benefit to any officer or employee of the CLIENT in connection with the award or performance of this Agreement.

ARTICLE XII
INSURANCE

12.1 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall, at its own cost, obtain and maintain throughout the Term comprehensive general liability insurance with a limit of not less than TWENTY-FIVE MILLION PESOS (PHP 25,000,000.00) per occurrence, and such other insurance as may be required by Applicable Law, naming the CLIENT as an additional insured.

12.2 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall likewise maintain property or all-risk insurance in an amount of not less than TEN MILLION PESOS (PHP 10,000,000.00), and shall deliver certificates of insurance to the CLIENT within fifteen (15) days from signing and upon each renewal of the policies.

12.3 Failure to maintain the required insurance shall entitle the CLIENT to procure the same and deduct the premiums from any amount due to the SERVICE PROVIDER.

ARTICLE XIII
CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 Each Party shall hold the Confidential Information of the other Party in strict confidence, shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party, except to its Personnel and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

13.2 The obligations in this Article shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or by order of a competent court, provided that prompt notice is given to the disclosing Party.

13.3 The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for a period of three (3) years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE XIV
DATA PRIVACY

14.1 To the extent that the SERVICE PROVIDER processes Personal Data on behalf of the CLIENT, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall act as a personal information processor and shall process such Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the CLIENT and in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

14.2 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall implement reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect Personal Data, shall notify the CLIENT of any personal data breach within twenty-four (24) hours from discovery, and shall return or securely destroy all Personal Data upon termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE XV
INDEMNIFICATION

15.1 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the CLIENT, its directors, officers, employees, and clients from and against all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, fines, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorney's fees, arising from: (a) any breach of this Agreement by the SERVICE PROVIDER; (b) the negligence or willful misconduct of the SERVICE PROVIDER or its Personnel; (c) any claim by the SERVICE PROVIDER's Personnel for wages, benefits, or injuries; and (d) any infringement of Intellectual Property Rights by goods or materials supplied by the SERVICE PROVIDER.

15.2 The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense, and provide reasonable assistance at the indemnifying Party's expense.

ARTICLE XVI
LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

16.1 Except for liability arising from fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, data privacy violations, or the indemnities in Article XV, the aggregate liability of each Party under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid to the SERVICE PROVIDER during the six (6) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

16.2 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, special, or consequential damages, including loss of profit, revenue, or goodwill, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

ARTICLE XVII
FORCE MAJEURE

17.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations, other than obligations to pay money, to the extent caused by a Force Majeure Event, meaning an event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party, including typhoons, earthquakes, floods, fire, epidemics, war, civil disturbance, and acts of government, that could not have been prevented by reasonable diligence.

17.2 The affected Party shall notify the other Party in writing within three (3) Business Days after the occurrence of the Force Majeure Event, shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate its effects, and shall resume performance as soon as practicable. If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than sixty (60) consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice without liability.

ARTICLE XVIII
TERMINATION

18.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for a material breach by the other party that remains uncured fifteen (15) days after receipt of written notice. The CLIENT may also terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the SERVICE PROVIDER at least thirty (30) days prior written notice, in which case the CLIENT shall pay only for services actually rendered up to the effective date of termination.

18.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, is placed under receivership or rehabilitation, or ceases to carry on business in the ordinary course.

18.3 Termination shall be effected by written notice delivered in accordance with Article XXII, stating the grounds for termination and the effective date thereof.

ARTICLE XIX
EFFECTS OF TERMINATION

19.1 Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement for any reason, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall promptly return all property, documents, and Confidential Information of the CLIENT in its possession, and the CLIENT shall pay all undisputed amounts due for obligations performed up to the effective date of termination.

19.2 Termination shall be without prejudice to any rights or remedies accrued to either Party before the effective date of termination, including the right to claim damages for any breach.

ARTICLE XX
DISPUTE RESOLUTION

20.1 The Parties shall endeavor to settle amicably any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement through good-faith negotiations between their senior officers within thirty (30) days from written notice of the dispute.

20.2 Any dispute not settled by negotiation shall be referred to mediation under the rules of the Philippine Mediation Center, and failing settlement within sixty (60) days, to arbitration in Metro Manila in accordance with Republic Act No. 9285, the Alternative Dispute Resolution Act of 2004. The arbitral award shall be final and binding on the Parties.

ARTICLE XXI
GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

21.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

21.2 Subject to Article XX, any action to enforce an arbitral award or to obtain interim relief shall be filed exclusively in the proper courts of Pasig City, to the exclusion of all other venues.

ARTICLE XXII

22.1 All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, by registered mail, by reputable courier, or by electronic mail with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses below or to such other address as a Party may designate by written notice:

22.3 Notices shall be deemed received upon personal delivery, three (3) Business Days after mailing by registered mail or courier, or upon confirmation of receipt in the case of electronic mail.

ARTICLE XXIII
ASSIGNMENT AND SUBCONTRACTING

23.1 Neither Party shall assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other Party. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall not subcontract any part of its obligations without the prior written consent of the CLIENT, and shall remain fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

ARTICLE XXIV
RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

24.1 The SERVICE PROVIDER is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employer-employee relationship between the CLIENT and the SERVICE PROVIDER or its Personnel. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall have exclusive control over the means and methods of performing its obligations and shall be solely responsible for the wages, benefits, and statutory contributions of its Personnel.

ARTICLE XXV
ANTI-CORRUPTION AND ETHICAL CONDUCT

25.1 Each Party shall comply with Republic Act No. 3019, the Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act, and all other applicable anti-bribery laws, and shall not offer, give, solicit, or accept any improper payment or advantage in connection with this Agreement.

25.2 The SERVICE PROVIDER shall promptly report to the CLIENT any request for an improper payment made by any Personnel of the CLIENT. Any breach of this Article shall be a material breach entitling the non-breaching Party to terminate this Agreement immediately.

ARTICLE XXVI
MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

26.1 No amendment or modification of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by the authorized representatives of both Parties.

26.2 The failure of a Party to insist on strict performance of any provision of this Agreement shall not be construed as a waiver of that provision or of any other provision.

26.3 If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be replaced by a valid provision that most closely reflects the intention of the Parties.

26.4 This Agreement, together with its Annexes, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, proposals, understandings, and agreements, whether written or oral.

26.5 This Agreement may be executed in several counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Documentary stamp tax on this Agreement, if any, shall be for the account of the SERVICE PROVIDER.
