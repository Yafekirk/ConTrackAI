# Contract Template

Use this structure so ConTrack AI can extract fields accurately.

## 1. Contract Header
- Contract Title: `<Title>`
- Contract Type: `<Supply Agreement | Service Agreement | Lease Agreement | Other>`
- Effective Date: `<YYYY-MM-DD>`
- End Date: `<YYYY-MM-DD>`

## 2. Parties
- Client Name: `<Company>`
- Vendor Name: `<Company>`
- Vendor Address: `<Address>`

## 3. Scope of Work
Scope of Work: `<Detailed scope>`

## 4. Financial Terms
- Contract Value: `<Amount>`
- Currency: `<PHP|USD|EUR>`
- Payment Terms: `<Net 30 | Milestone | Monthly>`
- Penalty Clause: `<Details>`

## 5. Renewal and Termination
- Renewal Terms: `<Auto renew / Manual renew / No renewal>`
- Termination Clause: `<Details>`

## 6. Signatures
- Client Authorized Signatory
- Vendor Authorized Signatory
- Signed Date: `<YYYY-MM-DD>`
